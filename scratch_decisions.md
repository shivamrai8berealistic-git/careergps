
## DEC-021: Product Framing — Navigation vs LMS
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** Career GPS is permanently framed as a closed-loop career navigation system, not an LMS or course platform.
**Reason:** Users need practical execution to reach a target role, not academic completion. Learning is merely preparation; the goal is evidence and readiness.
**Alternatives Considered:** Building a standard course-catalog LMS.
**Why This Path Won:** An LMS doesn't provide real-time recalculation based on resume updates or market shifts. A GPS does.

## DEC-022: Validation Pricing Strategy
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** Validation is always free and role-specific.
**Reason:** Proving readiness is core to the navigation loop. Monetizing it creates friction against user progress. We monetize premium generation tasks, not the core loop.

## DEC-023: Route Alternatives Default
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** Fastest Route is the default; alternatives are optional and hidden under a toggle.
**Reason:** Prevent choice paralysis for new users. Presenting 4 distinct routes immediately causes overwhelm. 

## DEC-024: Career Knowledge Graph Backbone
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** AI personalizes routes based on a standardized Knowledge Graph; it never invents routes from scratch.
**Reason:** Ensures consistency, high quality, and eliminates hallucinated "ladder" routes that lack credibility.

## DEC-025: Proof Engine Integration
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** The Route Execution Loop must feed directly into the Proof Engine.
**Reason:** Validation alone is not enough; the user must generate public proof (projects, portfolio items) to increase Employer Confidence.

## DEC-026: Continuous Route Recalculation
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** The route recalculates continuously based on real-world events (resume updates, chapter completions).
**Reason:** Like Google Maps adjusting for traffic, the route must evolve as the user's Current Position changes.

## DEC-027: Route Carryover and Target Changes
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision:** Changing target positions archives the old route and generates a new one, but matching completed skills carry over.
**Reason:** Preserves history while preventing the active UI from becoming cluttered with irrelevant past goals.
